# Exercise evidence review (2026-11-21)

Every exercise in the program was reviewed for how well it builds its target muscle (the smaller-muscle review below was added the same day). Weak picks get a replacement, and the technique points that research ties to growth became the app's **growth cues** (`src/seed/growthCues.ts`). They show first in each exercise's Form sheet, and the first one shows on the logger card. The review used the user's logged data from 2026-09-25 to 11-11 for stalls and weekly volume.

## How to read it
- **Hypertrophy trial:** muscle size measured before and after training (MRI or ultrasound). This is the strongest kind of evidence here.
- **EMG only:** how hard a muscle is activated during a set. It suggests a direction, but activation doesn't prove growth. Picks that rest on EMG alone say so.
- Most trials are small (12–60 people), short (8–12 weeks), and often in untrained people. Treat the numbers as direction, not precision.

## What the evidence agrees on
1. **Long muscle lengths.** Loading a muscle in its stretched position is the best-supported technique lever:
   - seated over lying leg curls (Maeo 2021);
   - overhead triceps work over pushdowns (Maeo 2023);
   - standing over seated calf raises (Kinoshita 2023);
   - bottom-range calf partials (Kassiano 2023);
   - stretched-range preacher curls (Pedrosa 2023);
   - full over half squats for the glutes (Kubo 2019).
2. **Full range ≈ lengthened partials** in trained lifters (Wolf 2023 meta-analysis; Wolf 2025). Full range with control at the stretch is the default. Stretched-range partials are an optional finisher.
3. **Machines ≈ free weights for growth** (Haugen 2023 meta-analysis). A Smith machine squat builds muscle about as well as a barbell squat.
4. **Weekly volume** is the main dose lever: 10–20 hard sets per muscle (Pelland 2025, in the spec). Four muscles were under the floor, so sets were added.
5. **Exercise choice matters less than these** for most muscles. Where two options grew a muscle equally, the program keeps the one already in use.

## Per-exercise verdicts
| Exercise (day) | Verdict | Growth cue | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Smith machine squat (Lower A) | Keep | Full depth, thighs below parallel | Full > half squat for glutes and adductors, quads equal (Kubo 2019); Smith ≈ barbell (Haugen 2023) |
| Leg extension (A, B) | Keep | Recline the seat; lower into a deep knee bend | Hip reclined grew the rectus femoris more (2025 trial, untrained men); knee extension grows the RF, squats and leg presses barely do (Maeo) |
| Seated leg curl (A, B) | Keep (best curl) | Sit tall or lean forward; full stretch | Seated > lying, +14% vs +9% (Maeo 2021) |
| Hip thrust (A, B) | Keep | Full lockout, controlled lowering | ≈ squat for glutes (Plotkin 2023) |
| Hip abduction (A, B) | Keep (fills a gap) | Full range, slow return | Squats and thrusts barely grew glute med/min (Plotkin 2023); targeted work grew glute min (clinical trial) |
| Standing calf raise (A, B) | Keep | Knees straight, 1–2 s pause in the stretch; bottom-half partials to finish | Kinoshita 2023; Kassiano 2023 |
| Cable crunch (A, B) | Keep | Flex the spine, hips still | Crunch thickened the abs most of the exercises tested (2023 ultrasound) |
| **Deadlift (Lower B)** | **→ Romanian deadlift** (deadlift stays as the alternate) | RDL: soft knees, hips back to a deep hamstring stretch | No trial shows deadlifts growing the hamstrings; hip-flexed long-length loading does (Maeo 2021, 2024) |
| Bulgarian split squat (B) | Keep | Deep; longer stride and lean for glutes | Depth (Kubo 2019); the lean cue is EMG only |
| **45° back extension (Lower A, new, 3 × 10–15)** | **Add** (hamstrings ~8 → ~11 sets/wk) | Hinge to a full stretch, rise to straight | Hip extension > Nordic for the hamstrings' long head (Bourne 2017) |
| Incline DB bench (Push) | Keep | 30–45°, deep stretch | Incline grew the upper chest most (training study, untrained); EMG agrees |
| Flat machine press (Push) | Keep | Full stretch to chest line | Full range at long lengths |
| Machine fly (Push) | Keep | Deep, controlled stretch | Full range at long lengths |
| Machine shoulder press (Push) | Keep (most optional) | Full range | Chest pressing already loads the front delts heavily |
| Lateral raise, machine (Push, Upper) | Keep; **Push 3 → 4 sets** (side delts ~9 → ~10) | Raise to about shoulder height, lead with the elbows, slow lowering | Cable ≈ dumbbell (Larsen 2025): a volume fix, not a selection fix |
| **Barbell shrug (Push)** | **→ DB shrug** (2 × 8–12; it was skipped 2 of 3 times) | Arms slightly out, shrug up, hold 1 s, full drop | EMG only: the arms-out shrug raised trap activity (2014) |
| Overhead cable triceps extension (Push) | Keep | Elbow overhead, deep stretch | Overhead > neutral arm (Maeo 2023) |
| Dip machine (Push) | Keep | Full depth | Full range |
| Weighted chin-up (Pull) | Keep (progressing at real bodyweight: e1RM 250 → 267) | Dead hang, chin over the bar | Vertical pulls activate the lats more than rows (EMG) |
| Machine barbell row (Pull) | Keep | Shoulder blades spread at the stretch | Full range at long lengths |
| **Rocking pulldown (Pull)** | **→ Neutral-grip pulldown** (flat for 5 sessions) | Arms straight at the top, no swing | Grip changes lat activation little (EMG); a stall variation |
| Incline DB Y-raise (Pull) | Keep | Light, thumbs up, no shrug | Highest lower-trap activity (Ekstrom 2003, EMG) |
| Rear delt fly (Pull) | Keep (best rear-delt pick) | Slight fixed elbow bend; move at the shoulder | Reverse fly > rows or pulldowns for rear-delt activation (EMG) |
| Face pull (Pull) | Keep | Pull to the face, rotate the hands back | Mainly rotator cuff and upper back |
| Preacher curl (Pull) | Keep | Full stretch at the bottom, no bounce | Stretched-range preacher grew the lower biceps (Pedrosa 2023) |
| **DB hammer curl (Pull)** | **→ Bayesian cable curl** (load went 45 → 35 lb) | Arm behind the body, full stretch | Bayesian ≈ preacher (2025): a change of stimulus, not an upgrade |
| Incline Smith press (Upper) | Keep | About 30°, deep stretch | As incline DB bench |
| Cable crossover (Upper) | Keep | Deep, controlled stretch | Full range |
| Machine row, upper (Upper) | Keep | Full protraction at the stretch | Full range |
| Lat pulldown (Upper) | Keep | Arms straight at the top | As above |
| **Cable rear delt fly (Upper, new, 2 × 12–20)** | **Add** (rear delts ~8 → ~10, twice a week) | As rear delt fly | As rear delt fly |
| Cable shrug (Upper) | Keep | Hold the top 1 s | EMG only |
| Incline biceps curl, machine (Upper) | Keep | Arm behind the torso, full stretch | Incline grew the upper biceps and preacher the lower (2025 RCT): they complement each other |
| **Straight-bar pushdown (Upper)** | **→ Overhead rope extension** (e1RM fell 186 → 153) | Upper arms by the ears, deep stretch | Overhead > neutral arm (Maeo 2023) |
| Machine triceps extension (Upper) | Keep | Slow return to a full bend | Full range |

Muscles under their weekly floor before the update (5-week averages): hamstrings ~8, rear delts ~8, side delts ~9, traps ~5 (band 6–16). Calves (~5) and abs (~3) are low by choice (exempt).

## Smaller muscle groups (abdominals first)
The program had one abdominal group (abs: cable crunches, 5 sets a week, exempt from "low") and nothing for the obliques or neck. All the additions below are **optional slots**: they count toward volume and progress when done, but aren't part of the required plan or a workout's planned-set count.

| Area | Evidence | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| **Rectus abdominis** | Crunches thickened it most of the exercises tested, 36% more than leg raises (Gomirato & Grenier 2023, ultrasound). Rollouts and hanging knee-ups gave the highest abdominal activation, with less hip-flexor work for rollouts (EMG). Squats and deadlifts activate it weakly, and no trial shows they grow it. A 9-week trial found "upper" vs "lower" ab exercises didn't grow different regions | **Ab wheel rollout 3 × 8–15 on Push and Upper (optional)**; abs now count toward "low". With the rollouts done, abs go from 5 to about 13 sets a week |
| **Obliques** | Side bridges, side bends and rollouts activate them strongly (EMG). There are no hypertrophy trials | **Cable woodchop 2 × 10–15 on Pull and Lower B (optional)**; Obliques tracked (about 7 sets a week when done) |
| **Neck** | Direct head-extension training grew neck cross-section 13% in 12 weeks; the same program without it grew the neck 0% (Conley 1997) | **Neck extension and flexion 2 × 12–20 each on Lower A and Lower B (optional)**; Neck tracked (8 sets a week when done). Start light |
| Visible abs | Ab training doesn't burn the fat over the abs (Vispute 2011; 2015 RCT), though a small 2023 trial disagrees | Body fat decides whether abs show |
| Transverse abdominis | A deep stabiliser; it doesn't change appearance | Not added |
| Forearms | No growth trials. Preacher curls grew the brachialis and brachioradialis more than incline curls (MRI, 12 weeks) | Covered by the preacher curl; not added |
| Adductors | Full squats grew them about 2× more than half squats (Kubo 2019); the adductor magnus is a main hip extensor in squats | Covered by full-depth squats, split squats and RDLs; not added |
| Serratus anterior | The push-up plus activates it most (EMG only) | Not added |
| Hip flexors, soleus, lower back, rotator cuff | Covered by leg extensions (and any leg raises), standing calf raises (the soleus grew equally standing or seated, Kinoshita 2023), RDLs and back extensions, and face pulls | Not added |

## Cardio (added the same day)
- Concurrent aerobic training doesn't compromise hypertrophy or maximal strength overall: [Schumann 2022](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40279-021-01587-7).
- Running, longer sessions and more frequent sessions interfere more, and cycling less ([Wilson 2012, via NSCA](https://www.nsca.com/education/articles/ptq/to-cardio-or-not-to-cardio-that-is-the-question-a-brief-review-of-concurrent-training/)).
- Lifting before cardio protects lower-body strength: [sequence meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28917030/).
- The app therefore puts cardio after the workout. It defaults to easy, low-impact machines (the bike on leg days) and ramps from 10 to 30 minutes.
